Quarterly customer feedback report. Compiled by Maria Sanchez (maria.sanchez@acme.com) for Acme Corp leadership. The team reviewed 1,247 support tickets and 89 product surveys, finding overall satisfaction held steady at 4.2 out of 5.

Customer John Doe contacted us on March 14, 2026 from his office in Boston. He can be reached at 617-555-0142. The issue centered on our export feature returning empty CSV files for accounts with more than 10,000 records. Engineering has flagged this for the next sprint.

Sentiment analysis across the quarter: customer satisfaction held steady despite the export incident. Most negative feedback clustered around onboarding friction rather than reliability.
